MUFG announces a JGB repo experiment on Canton Network

MUFG group companies announced collaboration with Digital Asset and Progmat on an experiment for on-chain JGB repo transactions using Canton Network.

Read the Japanese primary source ↗

Reviewed 22 September 2026 · The listing date may differ from the date on the linked document. Review date is separate.

What changed

  • One workstream will examine on-chain delivery-versus-payment settlement of JGBs and digital money.
  • The JGBs would retain their legal character as book-entry government bonds.
  • Tokenised deposits and stablecoins are being considered for the digital-money leg.
  • A second workstream will use Secured Finance's lending protocol to automate the repo transaction lifecycle.

Why it matters

The proposed mechanism links blockchain activity to updates of account-management institutions' transfer registers.

Watch next

  • MUFG plans discussions with the relevant authorities toward the experiment and subsequent implementation.
  • MUFG intends to work with domestic and overseas financial institutions and other market participants.
