MUFG firms announce a domestic tokenised-fund experiment1

The joint release scheduled a domestic tokenised investment-trust fund for establishment on 31 August 2026 as an experiment.1

Source listed 3 September 2026 · Reviewed 22 September 2026

The listing date may differ from the date on the linked document. Review date is separate. Source last rechecked 14 October 2026 and unchanged since review.

Japanese original ↗プレスリリース「本邦初の国内籍トークン化投資信託の設定・実運用環境における実証実験の開始」

What changed

  • The fund is intended to test investment management, trust administration and tokenisation infrastructure in a real operating environment.2
  • The fund's beneficial interests will not be offered or sold to external institutional or individual investors.3
  • The planned structure keeps the beneficial rights represented on certificates.4
  • The planned structure manages the register on-chain.4

Why it matters

The fund is intended to build practical knowledge of establishing and operating tokenised investment trusts.3

Uncertainties

  • The release does not guarantee future products, services or investment opportunities.5
  • The experiment's content or operation may change with changes in the legal, regulatory, system or technical environment.6

Watch next

  • The firms plan to expand the testing scope towards provision to external investors after the fund is established.7
  • The firms plan to expand testing of links with stablecoins or tokenised deposits after the fund is established.7
