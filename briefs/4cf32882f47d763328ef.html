MUFG firms announce a domestic tokenised-fund experiment

The joint release scheduled a domestic tokenised investment-trust fund for establishment on 31 August 2026 as an experiment.

Read the Japanese primary source ↗

Reviewed 22 September 2026 · The listing date may differ from the date on the linked document. Review date is separate.

What changed

  • The fund is intended to test investment management, trust administration and tokenisation infrastructure in a real operating environment.
  • The fund's beneficial interests will not be offered or sold to external institutional or individual investors.
  • The planned structure keeps the beneficial rights represented on certificates.
  • The planned structure manages the register on-chain.

Why it matters

The fund is intended to build practical knowledge of establishing and operating tokenised investment trusts.

Uncertainties

  • The release does not guarantee future products, services or investment opportunities.
  • The experiment's content or operation may change with changes in the legal, regulatory, system or technical environment.

Watch next

  • The firms plan to expand the testing scope towards provision to external investors after the fund is established.
  • The firms plan to expand testing of links with stablecoins or tokenised deposits after the fund is established.
