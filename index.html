The publication standard

Methodology
  1. 01

    The Japanese original stays canonical.

    Sources are archived with their page or heading locations, so every citation points somewhere you can check.

  2. 02

    Every claim maps to one passage.

    A separate bilingual pass checks that the passage supports the English sentence. A citation alone is not proof.

  3. 03

    A person decides.

    Nothing is published without an explicit approval. Rejected drafts stay private.
