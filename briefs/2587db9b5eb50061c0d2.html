FSA warns Izakaya Limited over unregistered cryptoasset exchange activity1

The Financial Services Agency issued a warning to Izakaya Limited in a notice dated 1 September 2026.2

Source listed 1 September 2026 · Reviewed 22 September 2026

The listing date may differ from the date on the linked document. Review date is separate. Source last rechecked 14 October 2026 and unchanged since review.

Japanese original ↗無登録で暗号資産交換業を行う者（Izakaya Limited）に対する警告書の発出の公表

What changed

  • The notice says Izakaya Limited conducted unregistered cryptoasset exchange business online with residents of Japan.1
  • The notice identifies Izakaya Limited as the operator of IZAKA-YA.3

Why it matters

The warning concerns unregistered cryptoasset exchange business conducted with residents of Japan.1

Uncertainties

  • The listed operator name and address may no longer be current.4
